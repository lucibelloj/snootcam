Privacy Policy

Snoot Cam by Joseph Lucibello. Effective November 26, 2026.

In plain words

  • Snoot Cam does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone. It has no internet features, works offline and sends nothing off your phone.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking, accounts or in-app purchases, and no code from other companies.
  • The camera picture and face tracking are used on your phone only to show the filters and make the photos and videos. Face data is not stored.
  • Photos and videos are saved only to your Photos library. The app can add to it but cannot see your other photos, and it keeps no copy of its own.
  • The microphone is on only while your child holds the button to record a video, and the sound goes only into that video.
  • The settings and the link to this policy are behind a grown-up check. Your child’s screen has no links, sharing or chat.

The full policy below says the same in more detail.

What we collect

Nothing. Snoot Cam has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. It does not send any information off your device, and it contains no code from other companies that could do so.

The app keeps a few simple settings on your phone, such as whether sound is on or which filters are hidden. These stay on the device and are never sent anywhere.

Camera

The app uses the front camera so your child can see themselves with a filter, live on screen. The camera image is used only to show the preview and to make the photos and videos your child takes. It is not sent anywhere.

Microphone

If you allow it, the app uses the microphone so videos can include sound. The microphone is on only while your child is holding the button to record a video, and the sound goes only into that video. The app does not listen at any other time, does not keep any sound for itself, and never sends it anywhere. If you don’t allow the microphone, videos are simply recorded without sound.

Face tracking and face data

To place filters on a face, the app uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking. This works out where the eyes, nose, mouth, and head are so the filter can follow them.

  • Face data is processed on the device, in real time, only to draw the filter at that moment.
  • Face data is not stored. It is gone as soon as the moment passes.
  • Face data is not sent off the device and not shared with anyone.
  • Face data is not used to identify anyone, for advertising, or for data mining.

Photos and videos

When your child taps the shutter, the photo is saved to your phone’s own Photos library. When your child presses and holds the shutter, a short video (up to 30 seconds) is recorded while they hold it and saved to the same place. The app asks only for permission to add to your library. It cannot see, read, or open the other photos and videos in your library. The app keeps no copy of its own and has no gallery: while a video is being made it exists only as a temporary file on the phone, which is deleted as soon as the video is in Photos (or if it cannot be saved).

Once a photo or video is in your Photos library, it is yours and is handled by your phone. For example, if you use iCloud Photos, your phone’s settings decide whether it is backed up. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by this app.

Third parties

There are none. Snoot Cam does not use third-party services, advertising, or analytics, and shares no information with anyone.

Children’s privacy

Snoot Cam is designed for young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing for a child to give away. The child’s screen has no links, sharing or chat. The settings area and the link to this policy are behind a grown-up check so children do not wander out of the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change would affect how information is handled, we will explain it clearly here.
