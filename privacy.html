Privacy Policy

Snoot Cam by Joseph Lucibello. Effective November 25, 2026.

The short version: Snoot Cam does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone. It works entirely on your phone and has no internet features.

What we collect

Nothing. Snoot Cam has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. It does not send any information off your device, and it contains no code from other companies that could do so.

The app keeps a few simple settings on your phone, such as whether sound is on or which filters are hidden. These stay on the device and are never sent anywhere.

Camera

The app uses the front camera so your child can see themselves with a filter, live on screen. The camera image is used only to show the preview and to make the photo your child takes. It is not sent anywhere.

Face tracking and face data

To place filters on a face, the app uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking. This works out where the eyes, nose, mouth, and head are so the filter can follow them.

  • Face data is processed on the device, in real time, only to draw the filter at that moment.
  • Face data is not stored. It is gone as soon as the moment passes.
  • Face data is not sent off the device and not shared with anyone.
  • Face data is not used to identify anyone, for advertising, or for data mining.

Photos

When your child taps the shutter, the photo is saved to your phone’s own Photos library. The app asks only for permission to add photos. It cannot see, read, or open the other photos in your library. The app keeps no copy of its own and has no gallery.

Once a photo is in your Photos library, it is yours and is handled by your phone. For example, if you use iCloud Photos, your phone’s settings decide whether it is backed up. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by this app.

Third parties

There are none. Snoot Cam does not use third-party services, advertising, or analytics, and shares no information with anyone.

Children’s privacy

Snoot Cam is designed for young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing for a child to give away. The settings area and the link to this policy are behind a grown-up check so children do not wander out of the app.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change would affect how information is handled, we will explain it clearly here.
