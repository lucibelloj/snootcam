Snoot Cam

A simple camera for little ones. Pick a silly face filter, tap the big button for a photo or hold it for a short video, and it is saved to the phone.

For parents

  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, and no code from other companies.
  • No accounts, no sign-up and no in-app purchases.
  • No internet features: the app works offline and sends nothing off your iPhone.
  • The camera picture and face tracking are used on the iPhone only to show the filters and make the photos and videos. The app keeps no copy.
  • Photos and videos are saved only to your Photos library. The app can add to it but cannot see your other photos.
  • The microphone is on only while your child holds the button to record a video.
  • Your child’s screen has no links, sharing or chat. The grown-ups area, with the settings and the privacy policy link, is behind a grown-up check.
  • You choose which filters your child can see.

Read the Privacy Policy or get help on the Support page.
