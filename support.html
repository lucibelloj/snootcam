Support

Snoot Cam is a simple camera for young children. They pick a face filter, tap the big button, and the photo is saved to the phone. There are no accounts and no internet connection is needed.

Where do the photos go?

Straight to the Photos app on your phone. Open Photos to find them. The app has no gallery of its own.

The camera does not work, or I tapped “Don’t Allow”. How do I fix it?

Open the iPhone Settings app, scroll down and tap Snoot Cam, then turn on Camera.

Photos are not being saved. How do I fix it?

Open Settings, tap Snoot Cam, tap Photos, and choose the option that lets the app add photos (“Add Photos Only”). The app never needs to see your existing photos.

How do I keep my child inside the app?

Use Guided Access, a built-in iPhone feature.

  1. Open Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access and turn it on. Set a passcode.
  2. Open Snoot Cam, then triple-click the side button.
  3. Tap Start. To leave, triple-click again and enter your passcode.

Does the app need internet?

No. It works fully offline and collects nothing. See the Privacy Policy.
